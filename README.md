## 🧠 About Me

I'm an Assistant Professor and researcher at **Universidad Rey Juan Carlos (URJC)**, where I'm part of the [GRAFO](https://grafo.etsii.urjc.es/) research group (*Group for Research in Algorithms For Optimization*).

My research lies at the intersection of **Artificial Intelligence** and **Operations Research**, with a focus on:
- 🔍 **Heuristics & Metaheuristics** for combinatorial optimization
- 🌐 **Social Network Analysis** — influence maximization & minimization problems
- 🔐 **Cybersecurity** — CTF competitions, network analysis & monitoring
- 📚 **Educational Technology** — tools that empower programming learning

I completed my PhD (2020–2024) with *Cum Laude* distinction and *International Mention*, including a research stay at the **University of Antwerp** under Prof. Kenneth Sörensen.
